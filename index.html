論 Kelsen 的權威概念
On Kelsen's Concept of Authority

法理學對「權威」的討論，往往隱而不顯。Kelsen 從未為權威單獨立論， 然而其規範體系的深層結構，究竟能否支撐一種「弱意義」的法律權威？ 本論文沿著 Hobbes、Finnis、Raz 的足跡，重返純粹法學的核心， 試圖給出一個更誠實的回答。

Jurisprudence has long neglected the question of authority. Kelsen never wrote a standalone treatise on it — yet his normative architecture may harbour a "weak" concept of legal authority. This thesis follows the footprints of Hobbes, Finnis, and Raz back into the heart of the Pure Theory of Law, seeking a more honest answer.

思想分期 · Intellectual Periods

Kelsen 思想三分期

Three Periods in the Development of Kelsen's Pure Theory of Law
本文將 Kelsen 的思想發展區分為三個時期，以掌握其權威論述的演變軌跡——從批判建構到懷疑，見證一位法學巨擘的思想轉型。
The thesis divides Kelsen's intellectual development into three periods to trace the evolution of his stance on authority — from critical construction to ultimate skepticism, witnessing the transformation of a jurisprudential giant.
c. 1911 — 1921
批判建構期
Critical-Constructive Period
以新康德主義為方法論基礎，Kelsen 建立純粹法學的初步架構。強調法與道德的分離，以「歸責」取代自然法的因果概念，奠定規範理論的起點。
Drawing on Neo-Kantian methodology, Kelsen laid the foundations of the Pure Theory. He emphasised the separation of law from morality and substituted "imputation" for causal explanation.
HP 1911 方法二元論 歸責概念
c. 1921 — 1960
經典期
Classical Period
純粹法學的成熟階段。確立規範階層論（Stufenbau）、基本規範（Grundnorm）、動態與靜態規範體系，並提出充權（Empowerment）為核心規範形式。此期是論文分析的主要焦點。
The mature phase. The Stufenbau (hierarchical structure), Grundnorm, and dynamic/static normative systems were fully developed. Empowerment norms emerged as central. This period is the primary focus of analysis.
RR1 1934 GTLS 1945 RR2 1960 Stufenbau
c. 1960 — 1973
懷疑期
Skeptical Period
晚期 Kelsen 對自身體系提出根本性的質疑。對基本規範的本質、邏輯矛盾律適用於規範衝突，以及「應然」概念的非理性化，均轉向更為存疑的立場，使傳統權威論述更加困難。
The late Kelsen raised fundamental doubts about his own system. He questioned the Grundnorm's nature, the applicability of logic to norm conflicts, and shifted toward a more skeptical position on the "ought," making traditional authority even harder to sustain.

概念分析 · Conceptual Analysis

權威的四種分類

Four Classifications of Authority
分類一 · CLASSIFICATION I
神聖權威 vs 世俗權威
Divine Authority vs. Secular Authority
接近權威來源的分類。近代法學在去神學化的過程中，神聖權威逐漸退場，但「超越實證法體系的權威是否存在」的核心問題，依然保留。
A classification by the source of authority. As modern jurisprudence moved away from theology, divine authority receded — but the core question of whether any authority transcends positive law remains.
分類二 · CLASSIFICATION II
社會學上的功能性觀察
Sociological-Functional Classification
觀察實際運作中的「變型權威」。強調的不是規範性問題（應不應服從），而是社會現象的描述——權威如何在制度中實際發揮功能。
Focuses on "authority in operation" as a social phenomenon. The emphasis is not normative (ought one obey?) but descriptive — how authority actually functions within institutions.
分類三 · CLASSIFICATION III
正當權威 vs 事實權威
De Jure vs. De Facto Authority
針對權威本身性質的分類。正當（de jure）指在規範上有資格行使的權威；事實（de facto）則指實際被服從的權威。兩者的關係與張力，是法理學討論的核心戰場。
A classification by the nature of authority itself. De jure authority has normative entitlement; de facto authority is simply obeyed. The tension between them is one of jurisprudence's central battlegrounds.
分類四 · CLASSIFICATION IV
享有權威 vs 身為權威
Being in Authority vs. Being an Authority
Joseph Raz 的關鍵區分。「享有權威」（in authority）指藉規範授予職位所產生的制度性權威；「身為權威」（be an authority）則指因專業知識而被視為諮詢對象的實質性權威。
Raz's key distinction. Being "in authority" refers to institutional authority derived from a normatively-created office; being "an authority" refers to epistemic authority based on superior knowledge or expertise.
思想家比較 · Theoretical Landscape

四位法理學家的權威論述

Authority in Four Jurists: Hobbes, Finnis, Raz, Kelsen

Hobbes
Thomas Hobbes
1588 — 1679
主權論 Sovereignty
命令論 Imperative Theory
社會契約 Social Contract
核心主張 · Core Claim
統治權威結合了「統治權力」（Governmental Power）與「統治權利」（Governmental Right）。主權者憑藉強制力與正當性，使臣民慣習服從法律命令。
Governmental authority combines "governmental power" and "governmental right." The sovereign uses coercive force and normative legitimacy to habituate subjects to obedience.
關鍵難題 · Key Difficulty
法的連續性問題：若法律來自主權者的命令，原始立法者已故後，法律的效力根源何在？Hobbes 的回應是：法律的效力來自「現在的權威承認」，而非最初立法者的命令。
The problem of legal continuity: if law derives from sovereign commands, what grounds it after the original legislator dies? Hobbes answers: law's validity rests on current authoritative recognition, not the original command.
對 Kelsen 的啟示 · Relevance to Kelsen
Hobbes 的主權論與法的連續性問題，直接呼應了 Kelsen 對「規範效力鏈」的思考——效力最終需要一個無法再向上回溯的起點。
Hobbes's sovereignty theory and continuity problem directly anticipate Kelsen's chain of validity — validity must ultimately rest on a point that cannot be further justified.
Finnis
John Finnis
1940 —
自然法 Natural Law
合作問題 Coordination
共同善 Common Good
核心主張 · Core Claim
人類的缺陷使權威成為必要。當群體越致力於共同善，權威與規則的需求反而越強——協調合作問題需要一個能快速且確定性作出決定的機構或個人。
Human limitations make authority necessary. The more a community pursues the common good, the greater the need for authority — coordination problems require an institution capable of reaching quick, certain decisions.
慣習成型與權威 · Customary Formation
Finnis 試圖用「慣習成型過程」說明權威的起源，但承認這個過程需要某種程度的意見合致，使它在邏輯上呈現笨拙——任何致力共同善的社群，最終都需要一個以上的明確決策者。
Finnis uses "customary formation" to explain the genesis of authority but admits the process requires a degree of consensus, making it logically awkward — any community truly pursuing the common good ultimately needs explicit decision-makers.
對 Kelsen 的啟示 · Relevance to Kelsen
Finnis 的「合作問題」框架提醒我們：即使在 Kelsen 的純規範體系中，也潛藏著對「協調秩序需要某種授權者」的預設。
Finnis's coordination framework reminds us that even within Kelsen's purely normative system, there lurks an implicit assumption that ordered coordination requires some kind of authoriser.
Raz
Joseph Raz
1939 — 2022
服務理論 Service Conception
先制論點 Preemption
依賴論點 Dependence
核心主張 · Core Claim
法律宣稱一種合法性權威。其服務理論包含四個相互連結的論點：簡單解釋、依賴論點、規範證成論點、先制論點。合法性權威的指令是行動的「排他理據」，替代而非疊加於主體的自身判斷。
Law claims legitimate authority. Raz's Service Conception comprises four interlinked theses. A legitimate authority's directives are "exclusionary reasons" — they replace, not supplement, the subject's own judgment.
對 Kelsen 的主要挑戰 · Challenge to Kelsen
若採 Raz 式的「先制論點」，法律必須能對主體發出具有排他性拘束力的指令，且此指令源自一個有正當性的權威者。然而 Kelsen 的純粹法學中，是否存在這樣的「有正當性的授權者」？這正是本論文的核心問題。
If Raz's preemption thesis is correct, law must issue binding exclusionary directives from a legitimately authorised source. But does such a legitimately authorised entity exist within Kelsen's Pure Theory? This is the thesis's central question.
Kelsen
Hans Kelsen
1881 — 1973
純粹法學 Pure Theory
充權規範 Empowerment
基本規範 Grundnorm
本文的核心主張 · Thesis's Core Claim
Kelsen 並未獨立論述「權威」，但其規範體系——尤其是充權規範（Empowerment Norms）與動態規範階層——確實蘊含一種「弱意義」的法律權威概念。這種弱意義的權威，指的是：規範藉由充權授予某人制定另一規範的能力，此即一種授權者（Authoriser）的存在。
Kelsen never wrote directly about authority, but his normative architecture — particularly Empowerment Norms and the dynamic Stufenbau — does harbour a "weak" concept of legal authority: norms empower persons to create further norms, constituting a weak sense of an "authoriser."
為何不是「強意義」的權威 · Why Not "Strong" Authority
Kelsen 的規範體系逐漸朝向「客觀化的充權建構」邁進，愈益去除傳統上「命令者—服從者」的人格性關係。因此，其架構至多只能支持弱意義的權威，而無法支撐如 Raz 式或 Hobbes 式的傳統合法性權威論述。
Kelsen's normative system progressively moved toward "objectified empowerment construction," increasingly eliminating the personal commander-subject relationship. Hence, it can at most support a weak form of authority — not the traditional legitimate authority of Raz or Hobbes.
Raz 服務理論 · Raz's Service Conception

Raz 的四個論點

The Four Theses of Raz's Service Conception of Authority
一
簡單解釋
Simple Thesis
「X 擁有對 Y 的權威，如果他說『讓 Y 做ψ』，而這是 Y 做ψ的行動理據。」Raz 的出發點：權威的指令本身就是一種獨立的行動理由。
"X has authority over Y if X's saying 'let Y do ψ' is itself a reason for Y to do ψ." Authority's directives are independent reasons for action.
二
依賴論點
Dependence Thesis
所有權威性指令，應大部分奠基於適用於命令對象的「依賴理據」——這些理據是獨立於命令本身、在命令所涵蓋情境下直接適用的理由。
Authoritative directives should be grounded in "dependent reasons" — reasons that independently apply to the subjects in situations covered by the directive.
三
規範證成論點
Normative Justification Thesis
建立合法性權威的規範性方式：若接受權威指令，主體能比直接依自身判斷行動更符合適用於他的那些依賴理據，則權威即具有正當性。
Authority is legitimate when accepting its directives helps subjects better conform to the dependent reasons that apply to them — better than acting on their own judgment.
四
先制論點
Preemptive Thesis
權威的指令不是疊加於其他理據之上，而是排除並「取代」某些理據——成為一種「排他理據」（Exclusionary Reason）。這是理解 Kelsen 規範體系與傳統權威關係的關鍵張力所在。
Authoritative directives do not add to existing reasons — they replace and "preempt" them, functioning as "exclusionary reasons." This is the key tension with Kelsen's normative system.
Raz 認為，若採先制論點，法律必須對主體發出「具有排他性拘束力」的指令。然而 Kelsen 的充權規範是「授予能力」而非「下命令」，這使得兩者之間存在根本的概念落差。
Under Raz's preemption thesis, law must issue directives with binding exclusionary force. But Kelsen's empowerment norms "confer capacity" rather than "issue commands" — a fundamental conceptual gap between the two frameworks.
本文的回應 · THE THESIS'S RESPONSE
對 Raz 式的「強意義」權威而言，Kelsen 的充權規範體系無法提供完整支撐。但這並不等於 Kelsen 完全沒有權威概念——充權規範仍然形成一種「弱意義的授權者關係」，使得法律體系內部存在一種非個人化的規範性權威。
For Raz-style "strong" authority, Kelsen's system cannot provide full support. But this does not mean Kelsen has no authority concept — his empowerment norms still constitute a "weak" authoriser-relationship, yielding a depersonalised normative authority within the legal system.
規範階層 · Hierarchical Normative Structure

Kelsen 的規範階層論

The Stufenbau: Kelsen's Hierarchical Structure of Legal Norms
「規範階層與機構階層，不會是平行的兩個系統，而是單一的效力鍊。因此它們最終可以回溯到基本規範。基本規範決定法律權力，而不是法律權力決定基本規範。」
"The norm-hierarchy and institution-hierarchy are not two parallel systems but a single chain of validity. They ultimately trace back to the Grundnorm. The basic norm determines legal power, not the other way around." — Ch. 3, thesis abstract

效力鍊的意義 · THE SIGNIFICANCE OF THE CHAIN OF VALIDITY
整個 Stufenbau 由「充權」（Empowerment）的概念貫穿：每一層規範都由上一層規範充權制定，形成動態的規範效力鏈。這種結構就是 Kelsen「弱意義權威」的所在——並非人格化的命令者，而是規範間的授權關係。
The entire Stufenbau is threaded through by the concept of "empowerment": each layer is authorised to create norms by the layer above, forming a dynamic chain of validity. This structure is where Kelsen's "weak authority" resides — not a personalised commander, but the authorising relationship between norms.
基本規範的困境 · GRUNDNORM DILEMMA
若基本規範是「擬制」（fiction）或純粹假設，它還能作為傳統意義上的「合法性權威」嗎？懷疑期的 Kelsen 對此給出了否定的答案——這正是其體系最終只能支持「弱意義」而非「傳統意義」權威的深層原因。
If the Grundnorm is a "fiction" or mere hypothesis, can it serve as "legitimate authority" in the traditional sense? The late Kelsen's answer was essentially negative — this is the deep reason his system can ultimately support only "weak" rather than "traditional" authority.
晚期規範論 · Late Kelsen's Norm Theory

規範的四種功能

Four Functions of Norms in Late Kelsen

晚期 Kelsen 在《一般規範理論》（GTN, 1979）中將規範依其功能分為四類。其中「充權規範」的地位，是本論文主張 Kelsen 具有弱意義權威的核心依據。In the General Theory of Norms (1979), the late Kelsen classified norms by function into four types. The status of "empowering norms" is the central evidence for the thesis's claim that Kelsen has a weak concept of authority.

⊕
命令規範
Commanding Norms
命令或禁止某種行為。傳統法理學中最常見的規範形式，直接對應義務的概念。
Norms that command or prohibit behaviour. The most familiar form in traditional jurisprudence, directly corresponding to the concept of obligation.
⊖
廢除規範
Derogating Norms
廢止、撤銷或終止另一規範效力的規範。展示了規範體系的動態性：規範可以被合法地消滅。
Norms that repeal, revoke, or terminate the validity of another norm. They demonstrate the dynamic character of the normative system — norms can be legitimately extinguished.
⊘
允許規範
Permitting Norms
允許某種行為，通常是對命令規範或禁止的部分解除。在 Kelsen 的晚期著作中，允許規範的地位與性質是爭議焦點之一。
Norms that permit behaviour, typically by partially lifting a command or prohibition. In late Kelsen, the status and nature of permitting norms was a contested topic.
⊛
充權規範
Empowering Norms
授予某人制定規範的能力或「法律權力」（Legal Power）。這是 Kelsen 規範體系中最具特色的形式，也是論文主張 Kelsen 具有「弱意義授權者」的核心：規範授予能力，即創造出一種非個人化的「授權者」關係。
Norms that confer upon a person the capacity or "legal power" to create norms. This is Kelsen's most distinctive normative form, and the core of the thesis's claim: by conferring capacity, norms create a depersonalised "authoriser-relationship" — the basis of weak authority.
效力論 · Theory of Validity

效力的三種型態

Three Types of Legal Validity in Kelsen
第一型
系統效力
Systemic Validity
規範屬於某一法律體系，意即其制定符合該體系中更高規範的充權條件。這是 Kelsen 體系中最核心的效力概念：效力等同於存在。
A norm belongs to a legal system — meaning its creation conforms to the empowerment conditions of higher norms within that system. This is Kelsen's central validity concept: validity equals existence.
Kelsen 主要採取 · Kelsen's Primary
第二型
事實效力
Factual Validity
規範實際上被遵守或執行。對 Kelsen 而言，這是一個「實效」（Efficacy）問題，而非效力問題。法律規範的效力不取決於實際遵守，但法律體系整體需要一定程度的實效。
A norm is actually obeyed or enforced. For Kelsen, this is a matter of "efficacy," not validity. A norm's validity does not depend on compliance, but the legal order as a whole requires some degree of efficacy.
Kelsen 區分為「實效」 · Distinguished as Efficacy
第三型
價值論效力
Axiological Validity
規範在道德上是正當的或有約束力的。Kelsen 的法律實證主義拒絕將效力建立於此型態上——法律效力獨立於道德判斷，這正是純粹法學的核心立場。
A norm is morally justified or binding. Kelsen's legal positivism explicitly rejects grounding validity here — legal validity is independent of moral judgment. This is the core stance of the Pure Theory.
Kelsen 明確拒絕 · Kelsen Explicitly Rejects
結論 · Conclusions

本論文的四個結論

Four Conclusions of the Thesis
Kelsen 的規範體系並非沒有「權威」，而是它所蘊含的權威，只能是一種去個人化的、規範性的「弱意義」——足以使法律體系運作，卻不足以成為 Raz 或 Hobbes 意義下的傳統合法性權威。
Kelsen's normative system is not without "authority" — but the authority it harbours can only be a depersonalised, normative "weak" form. Sufficient to keep the legal system running; insufficient to constitute traditional legitimate authority in the sense of Raz or Hobbes.
01
傳統權威論述無法提供正確的方式
Traditional conceptions of authority cannot provide the right framework for Kelsen
Hobbes 的主權命令論、Finnis 的合作問題自然法，以及 Raz 的服務理論，都無法直接套用於 Kelsen 的規範架構。這些傳統論述預設了一個「有正當性的命令者」，然而 Kelsen 的體系試圖從規範本身出發，排除人格化的授權者。
Hobbes's sovereign command theory, Finnis's natural law of coordination, and Raz's service conception all fail to map directly onto Kelsen's normative architecture. These traditional accounts presuppose a "legitimately authorised commander" — precisely what Kelsen's system tries to eliminate through depersonalised norm-creation.
02
Kelsen 具有法律權威概念
Kelsen does possess a concept of legal authority
儘管 Kelsen 未曾單獨論述「權威」，但透過規範體系的動態結構——尤其是充權規範的授予能力——可以辨識出一種弱意義的法律授權者概念。區分「法官命令」與「匪徒命令」的正是這種規範性充權，而非訴諸人格化的主權者。
Although Kelsen never wrote directly about authority, through the dynamic structure of his normative system — especially the capacity-conferring role of empowerment norms — a weak concept of legal authorisation can be identified. What distinguishes a judge's order from a bandit's command is precisely this normative empowerment, not an appeal to a personalised sovereign.
03
規範架構與充權規範可以支持弱意義的權威概念
The normative framework and empowerment norms support a weak reading of authority
Kelsen 規範體系的中心——充權規範與動態 Stufenbau——形成一種「去個人化的授權者關係」：規範充權某人制定規範，此即弱意義的權威。這種弱意義的權威足以使法律體系運作，並解釋法律義務的獨特性格。
The core of Kelsen's normative system — empowerment norms and the dynamic Stufenbau — forms a "depersonalised authoriser-relationship": norms empower persons to create norms, constituting weak authority. This weak authority is sufficient to operate the legal system and explain the distinctive character of legal obligation.
04
Kelsen 的論述較無法支持強閱讀或傳統意義的合法性權威
Kelsen's framework cannot sustain a "strong reading" or traditional legitimate authority
Kelsen 規範體系朝向「客觀化充權建構」的發展方向，使其逐漸無法支撐「命令者—服從者」的人格性授權關係。晚期 Kelsen 對基本規範本質的懷疑，更進一步封閉了傳統合法性權威論述的出路，確認弱意義的權威是 Kelsen 體系的最大限度。
The trajectory of Kelsen's normative system toward "objectified empowerment construction" progressively closes off the personalised commander-subject relationship required for strong authority. The late Kelsen's doubts about the Grundnorm further foreclose the path to traditional legitimate authority, confirming that weak authority is the maximum the Kelsenian system can sustain.
